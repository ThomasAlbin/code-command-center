## How to get one

Commercial licenses are available from the copyright holder, Thomas Albin.

Open an issue in this project's GitHub repository with the title "Commercial license", and say who would use the software and on how many Macs. Issues are public, so leave out anything confidential; the details are then settled in private. You can also reach the owner of the repository through the contact on their GitHub profile.

Written consent for a particular use, given by the copyright holder, counts as a license for that use.
